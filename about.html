How a story gets made

Every piece goes through the same four stages, and we keep what each one looks like along the way. That record is the magazine.

  1. Pitch

    One sentence on what the story is and why it matters now.

  2. Draft

    The whole thing, badly. Nobody is allowed to apologize for it.

  3. Cut

    An editor marks it up. Most of the first draft goes away here.

  4. Print

    The final version runs next to the page it started as.

No extensions. Ask anyway.

How to submit

  1. Pick something unfinished. One paragraph is enough.
  2. Keep every version you have, even the embarrassing ones.
  3. Bring them to the newsroom before the deadline.
